# Education

Education policy, school choice, pedagogy, and the economics of human capital formation.

## Articles
- [[la-zones-of-choice]] — LA's Zones of Choice program shows within-district public school competition improves achievement and college enrollment, especially for disadvantaged students
- [[relative-age-effects]] — being oldest in a school cohort causally raises social capital (Jacob & Bailey 2026 fuzzy-RD on 33M Facebook users): relatively older boys make 11% more friends, are 42% more likely to be class president, and sort into management/entrepreneurship as adults — a social-capital, not human-capital, mechanism
- [[ai-in-higher-education]] — how undergraduates use AI and what they think it does to learning and jobs (Sacerdote & Parris-Bacon's 2026 Dartmouth AI Survey, 2,536 students): 17% admit drafting essays with AI but say 63% of peers do, 92% say AI harms writing, 70% say take-home work is now unfair, 86% are self-taught, and opinion differs more by discipline than by class year
