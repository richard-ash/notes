# Business > Marketing

Advertising theory, brand strategy, and the mechanisms of consumer persuasion.

## Articles

- [[category-creation]] — Butterfield's 2013 Slack memo: when no one is shopping for what you've built, sell the behavioral transformation (horseback riding) rather than the product (saddles); five operational principles for marketing from both ends pre-PMF
- [[credibility-based-selling]] — Naval (2026) and HN's goatherders (2020) converge on "sales is a byproduct of credibility/usefulness, not tactics": Naval's eight steady-state moves (yes-and, charisma as confidence+love, lead-don't-manage, hunt-together stag hunts, feed-your-obsessions, fundraise on internal-excitement, walk away from bad deals, focus on upside not splits) + goatherders' cold-start protocol (four-motivations taxonomy time/money/sex/peace-of-mind, sell aspirin not vitamins, write birthday cards, introduce people, expect nothing in return)
- [[consultative-selling]] — Victor Cheng's PCNS framework (Problem, Consequences, Needs, Solution; "Please Close Numerous Sales"): three elicitation steps before any pitch, "your #1 competitor is doing nothing," the "quote the buyer's own words back" tactic, selling-as-transfer-of-confidence and congruence-based integrity, the win-lose-is-worse-than-lose-lose outcome ranking, and edu-selling as the method for manufacturing the credibility [[credibility-based-selling]] takes as given
- [[cpg-brand-building]] — Oza (CAVU) & Karp (HumanCo/Hu) playbook for building and investing in consumer brands: brand = pricing power + repeats + warm association ("do they make epic shit"); the three-phase model (due-diligence real-founder-story/real-need/50%+ margins → build team-IQ+EQ/route-to-market/packaging → turbocharge with influencers, never the foundation); route-to-market as deliberate scarcity (Chanel/Hermès/Apple never discount, Hu kept demand ahead of supply); the fanatical-few / one-in-ten (reach ≠ influence); celebrity turbocharges-not-saves (Aniston/Smartwater, 50 Cent/Vitaminwater, JT/Bai) with fit + authenticity; steak+sizzle; the "better for you" GenZennial shift; extensibility as the exit-multiple driver (Casamigos→Diageo 25×, overpay-for-winners); and functional-evolution-as-durable-DNA (Nike evolves, Coke is static) — with temporal notes on Peloton's collapse and Hu's 2021 sale to Mondelez
- [[cultural-imprinting]] — Simler's theory that brand ads work by altering shared cultural meanings (enabling social signaling) rather than by conditioning individual emotions
- [[distribution-strategy]] — Griffin's curation of Thiel/Andreessen/Bezos/Wilson/Gurley/Ellison on why poor distribution (not poor product) is the #1 cause of business failure, plus Horowitz's `f(p,t)=c` channel-design framework and the product/target matrix that determines viable channel shape
- [[doorman-fallacy]] — Sutherland's name for replacing a human with technology without consequence: the role gets defined down to its measurable function and all unmeasured value (error-handling, discretion, social presence) is deleted for free — illustrated by rozumem's QR-code-brunch where self-pay apps destroyed parallelism, coordination, and discretion that human service had absorbed
- [[psychological-moonshots]] — Sutherland (Invest Like the Best, 2020): improve *perception* not physics — a psychological 10× (Uber cutting wait-uncertainty ~25×) is cheap/simple/low-impact where a physical 10× is expensive and bounded by physics; the maps-vs-miracles frame for why economics can't see it; the "element of absurdity" thesis (Red Bull/Dyson/Amazon Prime — if it made sense someone would've tried it); alchemy = reframing a weakness into strength (Cunard, "we're number two so we try harder," Five Guys hypothecated pricing); division of reputation (specialists trusted over generalists) and design-for-tails-not-average with the U-shaped luxury/bargain retail curve; marketing as fat-tailed explore-not-exploit (not marcoms); binary rules beat rules of degree; and dumb "stupid why's" that open the solution space (train overcrowding) — companion to [[doorman-fallacy]]
