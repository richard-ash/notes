## Articles
- [[decline-of-the-public-company]] — de Fontenay's 2017 argument that private-side deregulation (Reg D, Rule 144A, §3(c)(7), the JOBS Act's 500→2,000 trigger) cut the benefit of going public, not just raised its cost: U.S. IPOs fell from 310/yr to 99/yr and listings from 8,025 to 4,101, while private firms free-ride on public disclosure and prices (competitive spillover, comps-based valuation), an A/B unraveling (200 → 195 → 190) that makes cheap private capital self-limiting; her verdict that the hybrid regime is worse than either pole, plus 2017–2026 temporal notes (unicorn IPOs below private marks, 2022 down rounds, 2020 accredited-investor and Reg A+ changes, the 2025 401(k) order)
