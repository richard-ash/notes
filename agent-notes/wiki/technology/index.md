# Technology
Technology as a force in the economy and society: platform and infrastructure cycles, Perez-style technological revolutions, the internet's maturation from access to use, and decade-scale forecasting; mirrors the personal vault's `technology/` directory.

## Articles
- [[apps-infrastructure-cycle]] — Grant & Grossman's (USV, 2018) "apps beget infrastructure" cycle (light bulb before grid, plane before airports, messaging before TCP/IP, Snapchat before Mux) with the adjacent-possible and development-vs-investment caveats, plus McCormick's (Not Boring, 2021) interface term ("web3 needs web3 interfaces", the four-property rubric, wallet-first and 3D-world guesses); temporal notes score the web3 interface forecast against the 2022 collapse and read ChatGPT-then-GPU-buildout-then-chat as the cleanest demonstration of the cycle
- [[distributive-era]] — Arthur's (McKinsey Quarterly, 2017) three morphings of the digital economy, external intelligence as the second great externalization after printing, the Keynes point ($73k per household), and the claim that the economy's binding problem has moved from production to distribution and therefore from economics to politics; synthesis against the 2026 labor-share and relational-scarcity debates, Housel's revolt reading, and the abundance agenda
- [[internet-access-history]] — Murphy's (Quartz, 2019) timeline from ARPANET's $700k Sigma 7 and "lo" through 56k dial-up, broadband and wifi, WAP/3G/iPhone, to 5G promises, compressed into a table; corrects the first-webpage date, and updates 5G (fixed wireless, not remote surgery), Loon-to-Starlink, the FCC's 100/20 benchmark and the shrinking offline share
- [[internet-maturation]] — Evans's a16z Summit 2018 keynote "The End of the Beginning": the access story is done and the use story is beginning, most people are online but most of the money is not, inverted assumptions (Yelp → DoorDash, listings → Opendoor), retail as logistics vs taste, "everything else" (cars, TV, money, death), and ML and crypto as new fundamental layers; scored through 2026 (ML paid off beyond the frame, crypto's 1993 analogy did not, Opendoor vs DoorDash)
- [[technological-revolutions]] — Neumann's (Reaction Wheel, 2015) exposition of Perez: five surges, technological systems and reverse salients, installation vs deployment, financial vs production capital, the 1950s as deployment, and his ten-year forecast that VC's job was done and lean would return; a 2015–2025 scorecard (the VC boom, transformers and the AI frenzy as a new irruption on top of a deployed ICT wave) and the deployment-vs-frontier toolkit rule
- [[technology-in-the-2020s]] — Dourado's New Year's Eve 2020 decade forecast (2% utilization-adjusted TFP as the end-of-stagnation bar; mRNA, CRISPR, plasma dilution, geothermal, EVs, autonomy, supersonics, Starship, Starlink, Mars by 2030, custom silicon, AR glasses, vertical farming) with a 34-row scorecard as of October 2026: direction mostly right, certification-gated timing wrong, learning-curve bets wrong, and LLMs as the decade's defining technology absent from the list
