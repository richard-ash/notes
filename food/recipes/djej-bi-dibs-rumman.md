# Djej bi Dibs Rumman (Pomegranate Molasses Chicken)

From Sunday Kitchen on Instagram.

## Ingredients
- 2 kg chicken pieces
- 3 tbsp pomegranate molasses
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 7 spice (baharat)
- Aleppo pepper
- Sumac
- Salt
- Cracked pepper
- 4 cloves of garlic, minced
- Extra virgin olive oil

### Potatoes
- 1 kg chat potatoes
- Salt
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Butter

## Method
1. Marinate the chicken overnight.
2. Add the chicken to a baking dish with all of the marinade and bake for approximately one hour at 180°C fan-forced.
3. Keep an eye on it after 45 minutes. If there is a lot of liquid, pour it into a saucepan and cook it on the stove until it reduces slightly.
4. Transfer the chicken to a serving dish and drizzle the reduced sauce all over it.

### Potatoes
1. Wash and cut the potatoes in half. Boil until tender.
2. Shake well and add to a baking dish with salt, olive oil and some butter.
3. Roast till golden.
