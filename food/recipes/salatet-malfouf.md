# Salatet Malfouf (Cabbage Salad)

From [Sunday Kitchen on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DHnP1LtzgSQ/).

## Ingredients
- 1/4 head of purple cabbage, finely shredded
- 1/2 cup currants
- 1 cup roasted walnuts, roughly chopped

### Dressing
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- Salt to taste
- 1 heaped tsp caraway seeds
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- Juice of half a lemon

## Method
1. Add all of the salad ingredients to a mixing bowl.
2. Heat the caraway seeds in a fry pan until fragrant and remove off the heat.
3. Add the remaining dressing ingredients to a small mixing bowl and add the toasted caraway seeds.
4. Toss the cabbage with the dressing. Serve immediately.
