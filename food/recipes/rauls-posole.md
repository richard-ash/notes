# Raul's Posole

From a printed card handed out by Rodriguez S&J Farm, New Mexico, tel. (505) 901-4446.

## Ingredients
- 2 lb pork shoulder roast
- 1 lb dried posole
- 2 to 3 tbsp New Mexico red chile powder
- 1 medium tomato
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper
- Chopped onion and oregano, to serve

## Method
1. Put the pork and posole in a pot with enough water to cover them and cook. Simmer until the posole is tender, about 2 hours.
2. Blend 2 cups water, the red chile, salt, pepper, garlic and tomato. Add to the pot.
3. Cook another 20 minutes.
4. Serve with chopped onions and oregano.
